Short Bio

HongningLiu is currently a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology. He received the master degree in Electronic Information from Dalian Minzu University, and the bachelor degree in Software Engineering from Harbin University of Science and Technology.

His academic interests include remote sensing image interpretation, computer vision, and signal processing.
