Short Bio

刘洪宁 is currently a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology. He received his master's training in Electronic Information from Dalian Minzu University and his bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Harbin University of Science and Technology.

His academic and engineering interests include software engineering, electronic information, computer vision, object detection, intelligent application development, and reproducible research workflows.
